**1. Website Analysis for Marketing Project**

*   **allworkss.in:** This domain is currently parked on Hostinger and doesn't have a live website on it yet. Will eventually be used for the main landing page or redirect to the app.

**2. Setting Up a New Meta Ads Manager**
A 5-step guide to starting fresh with a new Meta Business Suite and Ad Account linked to Instagram ID (`yasar.i.khan`):
1.  Ensure you have a dedicated Facebook Business Page for "Allworkss".
2.  Link your Instagram account (`yasar.i.khan`) directly to that Facebook Page.
3.  Go to business.facebook.com and create a brand new Meta Business Portfolio for "Allworkss".
4.  Add your Facebook Page and Instagram Account into this new Business Manager.
5.  Create a fresh Ad Account, set your currency/timezone, assign yourself full admin permissions, and add your payment method.

**3. Deleting the Old "YARSA STORE" Business Portfolio**
Steps to get rid of the old "YARSA STORE" portfolio:
1.  Click **Business info** on the left-hand menu.
2.  Click **Permanently delete business** in the top right.
3.  Follow the prompts to confirm (noting that it takes 24 hours to delete and requires all ad balances to be cleared).
