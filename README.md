# Research & Knowledge Base

Welcome to your structured knowledge base!

## Folder Structure

*   **Marketing/**: Keep your marketing notes, target audience research, and campaign ideas here.
*   **Documents/**: Store official documents, legal texts, or reference PDFs here.
*   **Strategies/**: Brainstorms, action plans, and execution steps.
*   **BusinessPlans/**: Core business model, financial plans, and pitch drafts.

## How to Sync

You can sync your files securely to your Private GitHub repository by running the script:
`.\sync.ps1` or `.\sync.ps1 -message "Added new marketing strategy"`
